01 / 12 · Brand foundation

What 1Trade is.
What it isn't.

A three-sided commodity venue connecting GPU datacenters, traders, and AI companies. Closest analogues: CME, Coinbase, ICE, Bloomberg Terminal. Not CoreWeave. Not Lambda. Not Together.

— Positioning

The commodity market for AI compute. Real underlying. Real liquidity. Real settlement.

The brand must feel institutional (Fortune 500 procurement and frontier-lab CTOs should trust it), technical (engineers should recognize craft and substance), financial (traders should recognize a credible venue), and modern (not bank-stodgy, not consumer-fintech-cute).

— Anchors and anti-anchors

Look toward

  • Bloomberg Terminal — dense, professional, information-rich
  • Stripe — calm confidence, elegant typography
  • Linear — modern, performant, dark-native
  • Vercel — minimal, technical, designer-credible
  • Polymarket — clean trading for non-finance users
  • CME web — institutional gravitas without being boring

Look away from

  • Robinhood — friendly green, consumer cheer
  • Coinbase consumer — playful blue, animation-heavy
  • AI-startup purple gradients — generic SaaS
  • Web3 neon — glitch, decentralization tropes
  • Bank navy + gold — old institution clichés
  • Hyperscaler PowerPoint — AWS / Azure corporate

— Brand promise

Every visual decision should reinforce: this is a credible market venue you'd trust with real money.

Custom letterforms or carefully-tuned typeface. Wordmark stands alone; possible monogram for tiny applications. Reference: Bloomberg wordmark, Mailchimp.

04 / 12 · Color

Tokens, not values.
Restraint, not range.

Near-black + warm off-white anchor every surface. Lime is the single brand accent. Positive / negative semantic colors are reserved exclusively for trading state.

— Brand

Brand accent — Lime brand.primary #C8F25C
Brand secondary — Orange brand.secondary #F5A623
Brand tertiary — Blue brand.tertiary #4A90E2
Brand support — Yellow brand.support #F5D24B

Lime is the primary accent. Orange, blue, and yellow are categorical accents for differentiating three or four parallel concepts (e.g., three product layers, four revenue streams) — never used decoratively.

— Surfaces

06 / 12 · Components

Square, restrained,
state-aware.

Sharp corners. Single primary CTA per surface. Every interactive element defines hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, and error states.

— Buttons · hierarchy

— Variants · default 40px height
— Sizing
— Trading buy / sell

— Form inputs

Number inputs use mono digits, right-aligned, with tabular figures. Focus ring is 2px brand accent with 1px offset — non-negotiable.

— Cards

Surface-flat

No elevation

Inline content blocks. No shadow, 1px border only when separation is needed.

Card-default

Subtle elevation

Standard card. 1px border + 2px shadow. Used for most product surfaces.

Card-prominent

Strong elevation

Modals, dropdowns, focused panels. 4–8px shadow, stronger border.

— Glass / frosted use

Where glass works

  • Modal overlays where order book context should stay visible
  • Sticky top nav over scrolling content (subtle blur only)
  • Hover popovers in trading UI

Where glass is wrong

  • Primary content cards — loses information density
  • Marketing hero — looks consumer-app
  • Anything text-bearing where contrast matters

— Status badges & pills

Live Pending Halted Paper

— Required states (every interactive element)

  • default — resting
  • hover — pointer over (touch devices skip)
  • focus-visible — 2px brand-accent ring, 1px offset (mandatory)
  • active — pressed (1px y-translate; never scale)
  • disabled — 40% opacity, no cursor
  • loading — spinner or skeleton, never shimmer
  • error — semantic.negative border + helper text

08 / 12 · Motion

Calm. Fast. Purposeful.

Every animation serves a function. Nothing slower than 500ms in product UI. Spring physics belong on consumer apps — this isn't one. Respects prefers-reduced-motion.

— Trading UI motion (unique constraints)

  • Price ticks: 200ms color flash (green up, red down). Never on every tick — only on the user's own positions or watchlisted assets.
  • Order book updates: visible but not distracting. No flash except on user's own orders.
  • Chart updates: smooth interpolation, never jumping. Hold the previous frame until the new one is fully drawn.
  • No decorative motion anywhere in trading UI. No hover wiggles, no parallax, no "reveal" on scroll.

09 / 12 · Imagery & video

No photography by default.

Direction A primary: zero photography, diagrams and type carrying the marketing surfaces. Direction B (datacenter / infrastructure) reserved for specific "underlying" contexts only.

— Strategy

Direction A · Default

No photography. Diagrams, charts, abstract illustration only. Reference: Stripe, Linear. Cheap, consistent, scales globally.

Direction B · Selective

Datacenter / infrastructure. Moody, low-light, hardware-focused. Used only for "the underlying" sections and partnership pages.

Direction C · Avoided

Editorial finance imagery. Too cliché — trading floors, skyscrapers, handshakes. Not for 1Trade.

— Image treatment

  • Subtle desaturation (10–15%) for consistency across sources
  • Aspect ratios: 16:9 standard · 4:3 editorial · 1:1 social
  • Text-over-image: solid color block over the text-bearing portion, never gradient overlay
  • Resolution: 2× for retina; AVIF / WebP for web

— Forbidden imagery

  • AI-generated images — creates "AI startup" association regardless of subject
  • Stock photography of "diverse team in conference room"
  • Generic futuristic cityscapes or data-flow abstractions
  • Cliché finance imagery — handshakes, bulls, towering buildings, dollar bills

— Video

ContextLengthStyle

Music: instrumental, modern minimal. No "tech startup energy" stock tracks. Subtitles mandatory.

10 / 12 · Voice & tone

Direct. Specific.
Confident. Technical. Honest.

A senior trader explains the venue to a colleague over lunch. Not a hype merchant, not a banking lawyer, not a friendly chatbot.

— Attributes in practice

— Yes · Direct

"H100 at $2.99/hr. Per second."

— No · Vague

"Discover blazing-fast GPU compute."

— Yes · Specific

"Order book depth: $50K within 1%."

— No · Generic

"Deep liquidity for serious traders."

— Yes · Confident

"The commodity market for AI compute."

— No · Hedged

"One of the leading AI compute marketplaces."

— Yes · Honest

"Paper trading v1, real-money testing in parallel."

— No · Misleading

"Real money trading available now."

— Words to favor

— Words to avoid

revolutionary · disruptive · game-changing · next-gen · cutting-edge · powered by AI · AI-driven · seamless · frictionless · magical · best-in-class · world-class · anything ending in -as-a-service

— Tone by context

  • Marketing headlines — confident, declarative, headline-style
  • Product UI labels — terse, technical, action-oriented (≤3 words where possible)
  • Documentation — clear, didactic, code-first
  • Error messages — what happened + what to do next; never blame the user
  • Trading interface — minimal language, maximum information density
  • Compliance / legal — precise, conservative

— Error message pattern

Yes

Order rejected. Insufficient balance: $1,200 available, $1,500 required. Add funds →

No

Oops! Something went wrong. Please try again later.

11 / 12 · Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 AA.
Baseline, not aspiration.

Compliance is the floor. The trading interface adds platform-specific obligations: directional shapes, screen-reader chart alternatives, multi-channel alerts.

— Targets

RequirementStandard
Body text contrast4.5 : 1 minimum
Large text / UI components3 : 1 minimum
Focus indicators2px visible ring on every interactive element
Keyboard navigationFull app navigable without mouse
Screen readerSemantic HTML; ARIA only where needed
Reduced motionprefers-reduced-motion disables non-essential animation
Color-blind safeNever rely on color alone for critical information

— Trading-specific commitments

  • Buy / sell distinguishable without color (/ shapes)
  • Order book readable in monochrome
  • Every chart has a data-table alternative for screen-reader users
  • Critical alerts (margin call, budget exceeded) use multiple channels: visual + sound + push

— Testing

  • Automated axe-core or Lighthouse checks in CI
  • Manual screen-reader testing for primary flows (signup, trade execution, billing)
  • Keyboard-only navigation testing for the trading interface
  • Color-blindness simulation review (deuteranopia, protanopia)
